Our Class Offerings

We offer a variety of professional services tailored to support your wellness journey.

Gentle Yoga

Perfect for older adults and beginners. This class focuses on slow movements, stretching, and deep breathing.

Vinyasa Flow

Designed for busy professionals seeking to build strength and sweat away the stress of the work week.

Restorative Yoga

A deeply calming practice using props to fully support the body, allowing for complete physical and mental relaxation.

Private Sessions

Receive personalized, one-on-one solutions tailored exactly to your physical goals and physical accommodations.

Pricing Overview

We offer flexible drop-in rates, class cards, and monthly unlimited passes. No online checkout is required; you can register and pay directly at our front desk before your first session.
