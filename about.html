Our Instructors

Our certified instructors specialize in making yoga approachable, safe, and calming for practitioners of all experience levels.

Accessibility Accommodations

We believe yoga is for everyone. Our studio space is fully wheelchair accessible, and we happily provide chairs, bolsters, and straps for modified, seated variations in all of our classes.

Studio Policies

To preserve our calm atmosphere, please arrive 10 minutes before class starts and turn off all cell phones before entering the practice room.

Guided Audio Practice
